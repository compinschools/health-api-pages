Alfred Personal Health Integration

This is a personal, single-user software integration built and used exclusively by its developer to retrieve their own health and fitness data (steps, sleep, heart rate, and related metrics) from the Google Health API, for display in a private personal dashboard and assistant application.

This application is not distributed publicly, does not accept sign-ups from other users, and is not used to collect, process, or share data belonging to anyone other than its single authorised account holder.

See our Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.
